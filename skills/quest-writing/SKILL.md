# Quest writing

Jk reads the saved Quest, not the conversation that produced it. Each rule below is what makes
the record stand on its own.

**Title** — name the outcome, and say what this Quest owns that its siblings do not. Three
coordinated audits titled "Audit all sites for remaining Vercel dependencies", "Audit BTG website
for remaining Vercel dependencies" and "Audit App for Gutters Vercel dependencies" left no way to
see which one synthesises and which supply evidence. A version suffix (`-v5`, `-v6`) distinguishes
nothing.

**Objective** — the first sentence is the goal and only the goal; the board truncates what follows.
Who asked ("Jk:", "User requests") is not the goal, and neither is the authorization. Keep the
quote, the authorization and the exclusions in later sentences. State a deadline as the condition
it depends on, not a timestamp nothing will mark stale once it passes.

**Steps** — a step title says what this task's work is and what will be checkable when it is done.
`Implementation` / `Verification` / `Integration` is a lifecycle template, not this Quest's plan.

`create` and `update` both refuse a bare lifecycle title, a bare lifecycle step title, and an
objective opening with the speaker — renaming a Quest into one is refused too. Everything else here
is yours to get right.
