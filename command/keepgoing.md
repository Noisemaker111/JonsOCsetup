description: Continue the authorized work to completion without re-planning
---

Continue the work already authorized in this session.

Do not re-plan, do not re-summarize what you have done, and do not ask whether to proceed. Pick up at
the first unfinished thing and carry it to the outcome that was asked for. Stop only at that outcome, a
real blocker you can state in one line, or an authorization gate (merge, publish, production, spend,
destructive).

If nothing is unfinished, say so in one line instead of inventing work.
